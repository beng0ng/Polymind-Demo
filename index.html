How to play
  • Win by taking Bardur's capital: move a unit onto it, then use Capture.
  • Stars arrive every turn (the +number). Spend them on techs, units and tile improvements.
  • Grow: move a unit onto a village and Capture it on your next turn. Fruit, hunting and farms add population; a full city levels up and gives you a reward.
  • Fight: select a unit, then tap a red target. The badge shows the damage you deal and what comes back.
  • Ruins (?) hold a random reward: a unit that starts its turn there can Explore.
